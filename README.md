# CSAT-AGENTS-PERFORMANCE-DASHBOARD-
An interactive Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) Performance Dashboard built to monitor, analyze, and visualize the performance of customer service agents.  The dashboards provides a centralized view of key CSAT metrics, enabling users to understand agent-level performance, identify trends, and gain actionable insights into customer satisfaction.

# Key Features
## Agent-wise CSAT Performance: 
Compare CSAT metrics across all agents such as Total survey, NPS(%), Promotors, Detractors and Neutral Counts, Total Calls (Valid, Invalid etc), Total Participations across all (%) etc.  
## Performance Tracking: 
Monitor individual agent performance over time 
## Interactive Filters & Slicers (Day & Month Wise): 
Allows stck holders to dynamically explore the data.
## Interactive Visualizations:
Explore customer satisfaction data through charts and KPIs.
## Trend Analysis: 
Identify improvements, declines, and patterns in CSAT performance.
## KPI Overview: 
Quickly understand overall customer satisfaction and agent performance.
## Data-Driven Insights: 
Support performance monitoring and informed decision-making.

# Objective
The primary objective of this report is to provide a simple and effective way to monitor customer satisfaction across individual agents, identify performance patterns, and highlight areas that may require further attention.
